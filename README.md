# MySafeBox Support

MySafeBox is a private, encrypted vault for your iPhone and iPad. Keep passwords, cards, IDs, bank details, Wi-Fi networks, and private notes in one place, protected by your master passcode and Face ID. Your vault stays on your device. There are no accounts, ads, subscriptions, or tracking.

## Getting started

1. Open MySafeBox and create a **master passcode** (at least 6 characters). Choose something you'll remember: it protects your vault and your backups, and it can't be recovered.
2. Optionally use **Face ID** (or Touch ID) for faster unlocking.
3. Tap **Add** to create your first item. Pick a category (Password, Card, Private Note, ID, Banking, Wi-Fi, or Other), fill in the details, and tap **Save**.

**Tips**

- Tap an item to view it; tap **Edit** to change it.
- Use the search field and the **Sort** menu to find items quickly. Search ignores capitals and accents, so "cafe" finds "Café".
- **Generate password** creates a strong random password, with the length and character types you choose.
- Add photos to ID and card items, or attach files and photos to other items. You can **Take Photo** with the camera or **Choose Photo** from your library.
- For a Wi-Fi item, **Show QR Code** lets someone nearby join the network by scanning it with their camera.
- Copied passwords are cleared from the clipboard automatically after 60 seconds.
- **More (…) → 5-Min Unlock** lets you return to the app within 5 minutes without unlocking again. Tapping **Lock** always locks immediately.
- **More (…) → About MySafeBox** shows the app version, the privacy policy, and the terms of use.

## AutoFill

MySafeBox can fill your saved logins in other apps and in Safari, and save new ones.

1. Open **More (…) → AutoFill** in MySafeBox, and follow the steps to turn on MySafeBox in iOS Settings (**General → AutoFill & Passwords**).
2. When you sign in to an app or website, tap the key or the suggestion above the keyboard and choose MySafeBox.
3. Unlock with Face ID or your master passcode to fill in the login.

**Show in Keyboard Suggestions** (in MySafeBox's AutoFill settings) controls whether your logins appear above the keyboard. Suggestions only ever include the website and username; passwords are filled in only after you unlock.

## Password Problems and leaked passwords

**More (…) → Password Problems** lists logins that need attention:

- **Expired**: items whose expiry date has passed.
- **Reused Passwords**: logins that share a password with another login.
- **Leaked Passwords**: passwords found in known data breaches.

**Check for leaked passwords**

1. Open **Password Problems** and tap **Check for Leaked Passwords**.
2. The first time, read how the check works and tap **Check**.

The check uses Have I Been Pwned. Only the first 5 characters of a scrambled (hashed) form of each password are sent, and the comparison happens on your device. **Only passwords are checked.** Usernames, emails, notes, and card or bank details are never sent. Results are saved in your vault, so warnings stay until you change the password or check again. Tap **Check Again** from time to time, because new breaches are added.

## Vaults

Vaults group items for a purpose, such as a trip or family documents. An item can be in several vaults; items in none are **Ungrouped**.

- **Create a vault:** More (…) → **Vaults** → **New Vault**. One vault is free; the Full Version adds as many as you like.
- **Show one vault:** use the **Showing** menu at the top of the main list.
- **Share a vault** (Full Version): open the vault → **Share Vault…**. Items you add to it later are shared too.

## Backup and restore

Your vault lives only on your device. Make a backup regularly so you don't lose it if your device is lost or replaced.

**Export a backup**

1. Tap the **cloud** button → **Export Backup to iCloud**.
2. Enter your master passcode.
3. Choose where to save the backup folder, for example in iCloud Drive.

The backup is encrypted with your master passcode. Without that passcode it can't be opened, by you or anyone else.

**Restore a backup**

1. Tap the **cloud** button → **Import Backup from iCloud** and choose your backup folder.
2. Confirm **Replace Local Vault**. This replaces the vault on this device with the backup's contents.
3. Enter the master passcode that was used **when the backup was made**. It can be different from your current passcode.

## Sharing with trusted contacts

Share selected items with people you trust, such as family members. Shared items are **end-to-end encrypted**, so only you and the people you choose can read them.

**Requirements**

- Both of you need MySafeBox and to be signed in to iCloud.
- Sending shares, inviting contacts, and creating groups need the Full Version. Accepting an invitation and viewing shared items are free.

**Invite someone**

1. Tap the **Trusted Contacts** button (two people). The first time, enter the name your contacts will see.
2. Tap **Invite** and enter the email address of the person's Apple Account, then send the invitation.
3. When they open the link and accept, they appear in your contacts.

**Verify the contact (recommended)**

Open the contact and compare the **safety code words** with them in person or on a call. If the words match, mark the contact as verified. This confirms that nobody is impersonating them.

**Share an item**

1. Open the item and tap **Share…**.
2. Choose people or groups, which fields to include (for example only the password), and how long to share it for.
3. Shared items show a small two-person icon in your list.

Edits you make are sent to your contacts automatically. They see the item in **Shared with Me**, read-only. To stop sharing, open the item → **Manage Sharing…**. Removing a contact stops all sharing with them, in both directions.

**Groups:** in Trusted Contacts, tap **New Group** to share with several people at once. Groups are only on your device; members don't see them.

## Recently Deleted and erasing your data

**Recently Deleted:** deleted items are kept for 30 days in **More (…) → Recently Deleted**, where you can restore them or delete them permanently.

**Erase All Data:** **More (…) → Erase All Data…** permanently deletes every item and attachment on this device, including Recently Deleted and all your vaults, stops everything you share, and removes your trusted contacts. You need your master passcode to confirm.

- Your master passcode is kept.
- This can't be undone. Export a backup first if you might need the data again.
- If you later restore a backup made before the erase, your items come back, but sharing doesn't resume automatically. Your contacts show as **Ended**; invite them again to share.

## Frequently asked questions

**I forgot my master passcode.**
On the unlock screen, tap **Reset passcode setup** and confirm with Face ID or Touch ID to create a new passcode. If you can't use Face ID or Touch ID, the passcode can't be recovered. For your security there is no back door, and the developer can't access or reset your vault. Backups can only be restored with the passcode that was used when they were made.

**Can the developer see my data?**
No. Your vault is encrypted on your device, and shared items are end-to-end encrypted. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Does MySafeBox sync between my devices?**
Not automatically. To move your vault to another device, export a backup and import it on the other device.

**Why is a password marked as leaked?**
It appears in Have I Been Pwned's list of passwords exposed in data breaches. It doesn't necessarily mean your account was hacked, but anyone can try that password. Change it wherever you use it.

**AutoFill doesn't offer MySafeBox.**
Check that MySafeBox is turned on in iOS Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords, and that the item has the website in its **Website** field and a password.

**My purchase isn't showing on my new device.**
Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account, then go to More (…) → Get Full Version → **Restore Purchases**.

**My contact didn't receive what I shared.**
Check that both of you are signed in to iCloud and online, and that the contact has accepted your invitation. Pull down on the main list to refresh.

**A contact shows as "Ended".**
The connection was removed, either by you, by them, or because the vault was erased. Invite them again to share.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
